Department of Chemistry, Indian Institute of Technology Bombay
Bioinorganic Lab of Prof. Arnab Dutta

I design bio-inspired molecular catalysts built from earth-abundant metals (iron, cobalt and copper) for clean-energy reactions: producing and oxidising hydrogen, and converting CO2 into formate using electricity or light. My work runs from mechanism, probed with in situ spectroelectrochemistry, through to working fuel-cell and electrolyzer devices.

Research

Rational design of molecular catalysts for electrochemical and photocatalytic small-molecule activation.

Hydrogen evolution & oxidation

Non-heme iron catalysts that both produce and oxidise H2 in water, immobilised on carbon electrodes and tested in working H2 fuel cells.

CO2 to formate

Bio-inspired iron and transition-metal catalysts for electrochemical and light-driven CO2 reduction in aqueous conditions, with mechanisms resolved by in situ spectroelectrochemistry.

Water-splitting materials

CoTe/NiS2 heterostructures as bifunctional electrocatalysts for overall water splitting, evaluated in an electrolyzer, and heterobimetallic complexes for cooperative H2 evolution.
